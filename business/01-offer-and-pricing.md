# Offer & Pricing — Syncpoint AI

## The Core Offer

**"Your practice answers every call, books every patient, and never misses a follow-up — even at 2am — for less than you pay for one day of front desk coverage."**

You are not selling "AI." You are selling a solved problem: missed calls = missed revenue. The average dental practice misses 30–40% of inbound calls. Each missed new patient call is worth $800–$2,500 in lifetime value. You answer that call.

---

## Pricing Tiers

### Tier 1 — Starter ($497/month + $797 setup)
Best for: Solo practitioner, 1 location, under 20 calls/day
- 1 AI phone agent (answers all inbound calls)
- Books/reschedules appointments (integrates with their scheduling software)
- After-hours coverage 24/7
- Handles FAQs (insurance, location, hours, emergency triage)
- Weekly call summary report
- 1,500 included call minutes/month (~30 min/day)

### Tier 2 — Growth ($697/month + $997 setup) ← *Lead with this*
Best for: 1–2 locations, 20–60 calls/day
- Everything in Starter
- Outbound recall campaign (AI calls patients overdue for cleanings)
- New patient intake via SMS after first call
- Google review request automation
- Monthly performance report with call recordings
- 3,500 included call minutes/month

### Tier 3 — Practice ($997/month + $1,497 setup)
Best for: Multi-location or high-volume (60+ calls/day)
- Everything in Growth
- Dedicated agent per location
- Custom emergency triage protocol
- Integration with practice management software (Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental)
- Bi-weekly check-in call (30 min)
- 7,000 included call minutes/month

## Guarantee Language

> "If your AI agent misses a call or books an incorrect appointment in the first 30 days, we'll fix it same day — or refund your setup fee, no questions asked."

This is safe because: (a) Bland AI has 99.9% uptime, (b) "incorrect booking" is rare and fixable within minutes, (c) you keep the monthly retainer either way.

## Objection Handling

**"Our patients want to talk to a real person."**
> "Completely understandable. Our agent sounds natural, handles the routine 80% of calls — scheduling, hours, insurance, directions — and transfers complex calls to your team instantly. Your staff spends their day on patient care, not on-hold requests."

**"We already have a receptionist."**
> "Perfect — this doesn't replace them. It handles overflow calls, after-hours calls, and the 'can I get your hours' calls so your receptionist can focus on patients who are actually in the office."

**"What if the AI says something wrong?"**
> "Every call is recorded and logged. You can review any call in 30 seconds. The agent only answers what you've told it to answer — it won't make up anything. Anything outside its script goes straight to your team."

**"How much does it cost?"**
> "Less than one day of front desk coverage per month. And it works every night, every weekend, never calls in sick."

## Upsell Path (Month 4+, Phase 2)

Once a dental client is on retainer and trusts you, offer:

- **Appointment reminder automation** (+$150–$200/month) — reduces no-shows by 40%
- **Review generation campaign** (+$150/month) — automated post-visit Google review requests
- **Reactivation campaign** (+$250–$300/month, one-time) — AI contacts lapsed patients
- **Full practice automation audit** ($2,500 one-time) — maps all manual workflows, delivers automation plan

These Phase 2 upsells move a $697/month client toward $1,200–$1,500/month without adding a new client.
