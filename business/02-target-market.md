# Target Market — Syncpoint AI

## Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

**Primary:** Independent dental practice, 1–3 locations, Southwest US
**Secondary:** Multi-specialty dental group (DSO affiliates with local autonomy)

### Qualifying Signals (prospect is a good fit if...)
- Google listing shows: "May not answer" or mixed reviews mentioning missed calls
- 3.5–4.2 star rating (common complaint: hard to reach, long hold times)
- 1–5 front desk staff visible on their website
- Uses Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, or Curve (common systems = easy integrations)
- No obvious chatbot or AI tools on their website
- Practice has been open 3+ years (stable, not a startup)
- Solo dentist or small group (not corporate chain — chains have centralized IT)

### Disqualifying Signals (skip these)
- Part of a large DSO (Heartland, Aspen, Pacific Dental) — IT decisions centralized
- Brand new practice — no call volume yet
- Oral surgery / orthodontics only — scheduling complexity too high for Tier 1

## Southwest US Market Specifics

### Texas (highest priority)
- 17,000+ independent dental practices statewide
- No specific AI voice agent regulations for scheduling calls
- Target cities: Dallas, Houston, Austin, San Antonio, Fort Worth
- Best sub-niches: Family dentistry, cosmetic dentistry in suburban markets

### Arizona
- High density of snowbird patients = high after-hours booking demand
- Target: Phoenix Metro, Tucson, Scottsdale
- Practices in retirement-adjacent areas especially underserved on after-hours

### California
- Largest market but most competitive
- Avoid LA/SF core — go for: San Diego suburbs, Sacramento, Inland Empire, Fresno
- Note: CA has stricter TCPA rules — ensure outbound recall campaigns have proper opt-in language

### Colorado
- Denver, Colorado Springs, Fort Collins
- Affluent suburbs = higher LTV per patient = easier to justify your pricing

### Nevada
- Las Vegas and Reno suburban practices
- High-volume practices due to tourism-adjacent patient mix

## How to Build Your Prospect List

### Method 1: Google Maps Scrape (Free, 1–2 hours)
1. Go to Google Maps → search "dental office [city, state]"
2. Filter: 3–4.5 stars, "independently owned"
3. Copy: Practice name, phone, website, owner name (from About page)
4. Look for reviews mentioning "hard to reach," "always on hold," "left a message"
5. Target: 20 prospects per city × 5 cities = 100 initial prospects

### Method 2: Apollo.io (Free tier, 50 exports/month)
- Search: "dentist" + state = TX, AZ, CA, CO, NV
- Filter: Company size 1–10 employees, "Owner" or "Practice Manager" title
- Export: Name, email, LinkedIn, phone
- 50 free contacts/month = enough for Week 1 outreach

### Method 3: LinkedIn Sales Navigator (if budget allows, $79/month)
- Search: "Dental Practice Owner" OR "Office Manager" + location
- Best for warm follow-up after cold email

## Prioritization Matrix

**Tier A (contact first):** Reviews mention missed calls OR "hard to reach" + 3.5–4.2 stars + 1 location

**Tier B:** No obvious phone complaints but 1 location, no chatbot visible on site

**Tier C:** Multi-location or 4.5+ stars (harder sell — they think they're doing fine)

Start with 20 Tier A contacts in Week 1. Do not spray-and-pray all 100 at once.
