## Demo Call Script (15 Minutes)

### Minutes 1–3: Qualifying
> "Tell me a bit about your practice — how many locations, roughly how many calls do you get per day, and how is your front desk currently handling after-hours?"

Listen for: volume, pain, awareness of missed calls.

### Minutes 3–8: Live Demo
> "Let me show you the agent in action. I'm going to call my demo dental office line right now — I'll put it on speaker."

[Call your Bland AI demo number. Let them hear the agent handle a scheduling request and a FAQ.]

> "What you just heard is a configured version for a dental office. Yours would have your practice name, your scheduling software, your specific services."

### Minutes 8–12: Fit Check
> "Does this feel like something that would actually solve a problem in your office?"

Wait for their answer. If yes → go to close. If hesitant → address one objection.

### Minutes 12–15: Close
> "Here's what getting started looks like. We do a 45-minute onboarding call, I set up the agent using your practice details, we test it together, and you're live within 48 hours. Setup is $997 and the monthly is $697. Is there a reason we wouldn't get started this week?"

## Weekly Outreach Cadence (Your 35-min Monday block)

1. Check Instantly dashboard → reply to any responses (10 min)
2. Mark replied/booked contacts in your tracking sheet (5 min)
3. Add 10–20 new contacts to the sequence (10 min, from your pre-built prospect list)
4. Review open rates — if below 35%, test a new subject line this week (10 min)

**Target metrics:**
- Open rate: 40–60% (dental office managers check email regularly)
- Reply rate: 5–12% (industry average for cold B2B is 2–4%)
- Demo booking rate: 2–5% of total sends
- Close rate from demo: 30–40% (pain is obvious, price is justified)
- To get 1 new client: Send ~50–100 emails
