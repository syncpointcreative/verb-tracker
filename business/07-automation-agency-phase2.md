## The Phase 2 Thesis

Your dental clients already trust you. They've seen your AI agent work. Now you're showing them the next layer: automated workflows that eliminate manual tasks their staff does every day.

This is not a separate business. It's an upsell to the same 8–12 clients you already have.

**Phase 2 revenue model:**
- Average Phase 2 upsell: $400–$800/month additional per client
- At 6 of 12 clients taking Phase 2 upsells: +$2,400–$4,800/month
- Total combined Phase 1 + Phase 2: $8,000–$16,000/month

## The 4 Core Automations to Sell

### Automation 1: Appointment Reminder System (+$150–$200/month)
**Problem it solves:** No-shows cost a dental practice $200–$500 per missed slot. Most practices do manual reminder calls.

**What it does:**
- 72 hours before appointment: SMS + email reminder with 1-click confirm/reschedule
- 24 hours before: final SMS reminder
- 2 hours before: if unconfirmed, AI makes an automated confirmation call
- No-show detected: auto-sends reschedule link + adds to recall list

**Tools:** Make.com ($9/month) + Twilio ($15–$30/month for SMS) + their practice management API or webhook

**Your build time:** 3–4 hours first client, 1–2 hours each repeat

**ROI pitch:** "If this saves just 2 no-shows a month at $400 average, that's $800 recovered for $175."

---

### Automation 2: Review Generation Campaign (+$150/month)
**Problem it solves:** Most dental practices have 50–200 Google reviews. Practices with 500+ reviews rank dramatically higher in local search.

**What it does:**
- 2 hours after appointment: patient gets SMS: "How was your visit at [Practice]? [Thumbs up / Thumbs down]"
- If positive: direct link to Google review page
- If negative: routes to private feedback form (captures complaint before it goes public)
- Tracks review count monthly, sends client monthly report

**Tools:** Make.com + Twilio + Google Business API (free)

**Your build time:** 2–3 hours

**ROI pitch:** "A practice going from 80 to 300+ reviews typically sees 30–40% more new patient calls from Google."

---

### Automation 3: Patient Reactivation Campaign (+$250–$400, one-time or monthly)
**Problem it solves:** Every practice has hundreds of patients who haven't been in for 18+ months. Reactivating one = $1,500+ lifetime value.

**What it does:**
- Extract lapsed patient list from their practice management system (they export as CSV)
- AI-personalized SMS/email sequence over 3 weeks: "Hi [name], it's been a while — is now a good time to schedule your next cleaning?"
- Warm leads automatically handed off to front desk to confirm
- Track reactivated patients vs. campaign cost

**Tools:** Make.com + Twilio + Claude API for personalization

**Your build time:** 4–6 hours (one-time build + run per quarter)

**ROI pitch:** "A 200-patient reactivation campaign that converts 15% = 30 patients back. At $300 average visit value, that's $9,000. Campaign cost: $300–$400."

---

### Automation 4: New Patient Intake Automation (+$200–$300/month)
**Problem it solves:** New patients call, leave info with front desk or AI agent, then staff manually enter data into Dentrix/Eaglesoft. Takes 10–15 min per patient.

**What it does:**
- After a new patient inquiry (from your voice agent or web form), patient gets a link to a digital intake form
- Completed form data automatically enters their practice management system (via API or CSV import)
- Patient insurance info triggers automated benefits verification request
- Patient gets confirmation + "what to bring to your first visit" email

**Tools:** Make.com + Typeform ($29/month for client) + PMS integration

**Your build time:** 5–8 hours (depends on PMS)

**ROI pitch:** "10 new patients a month × 12 minutes saved each = 2 hours of front desk time back per month. And no data entry errors."

## Phase 2 Sales Script (Upsell Conversation)

**Context:** You're on your monthly check-in call with a client who loves the voice agent.

> "One thing I've noticed in your call logs — you're capturing a lot of after-hours new patient requests, which is great. But I can see when we look at conversion rates that some of those aren't making it to confirmed appointments. Want me to show you an automation that follows up with those leads automatically within 2 hours — so your staff wakes up Monday morning and the appointment is already confirmed?"

Wait for their reaction.

> "It's about $200/month on top of what you're already paying. I can have it running within a week. The typical practice sees their new patient show rate go from 60% to 80%. Is that worth a try?"

**Key:** Lead with the problem you've already observed in their data. You have 6 weeks of their call logs. You can legitimately say "I noticed X in your data."

## Expansion Beyond Dental (Month 8+)

Once the dental playbook is documented and repeatable, apply the same system to:

**HVAC & Home Services**
- Same voice agent setup (after-hours booking, dispatch automation)
- Seasonal campaign automation (pre-summer AC tune-up, pre-winter furnace check)
- Review generation (same stack)
- Target: Phoenix, Las Vegas, Dallas suburban contractors

**Chiropractic / Physical Therapy**
- Very similar to dental (appointment-heavy, insurance-driven)
- Reactivation campaigns work especially well
- Compliance is simpler than dental (no PHI concerns with scheduling)

**Med Spa / Aesthetic Services**
- High ticket per visit ($300–$800+)
- Heavy social proof sensitivity → review generation is huge
- Post-treatment follow-up automation = loyalty/rebooking driver

**Expansion timing:** Start pitching one adjacent niche when dental is at 10+ clients and < 2 hrs/week. Use your dental case studies as proof.
