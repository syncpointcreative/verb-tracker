You are not selling "AI." You are selling a solved problem: missed calls = missed revenue. The average dental practice misses 30–40% of inbound calls. Each missed new patient call is worth $800–$2,500 in lifetime value. You answer that call.

# Target Market — Syncpoint AI

## Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

**Primary:** Independent dental practice, 1–3 locations, Southwest US
**Secondary:** Multi-specialty dental group (DSO affiliates with local autonomy)

### Qualifying Signals (prospect is a good fit if...)
- Google listing shows: "May not answer" or mixed reviews mentioning missed calls
- 3.5–4.2 star rating (common complaint: hard to reach, long hold times)
- 1–5 front desk staff visible on their website
- Uses Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, or Curve (common systems = easy integrations)
- No obvious chatbot or AI tools on their website
- Practice has been open 3+ years (stable, not a startup)
- Solo dentist or small group (not corporate chain — chains have centralized IT)

### Disqualifying Signals (skip these)
- Part of a large DSO (Heartland, Aspen, Pacific Dental) — IT decisions centralized
- Brand new practice — no call volume yet
- Oral surgery / orthodontics only — scheduling complexity too high for Tier 1

- 17,000+ independent dental practices statewide

- Largest market but most competitive

- Note: CA has stricter TCPA rules — ensure outbound recall campaigns have proper opt-in language

### Colorado
- Denver, Colorado Springs, Fort Collins
- Affluent suburbs = higher LTV per patient = easier to justify your pricing

### Nevada
- Las Vegas and Reno suburban practices
- High-volume practices due to tourism-adjacent patient mix

# Agent Configuration — Syncpoint AI (Retell AI)

## Platform Choice: Why Retell AI (Not Bland AI)

Bland AI was the early market leader but has meaningful problems for this use case:
- **Latency:** 800ms–2 second response time causes callers to talk over the agent, breaking the "sounds natural" pitch
- **Support:** Multiple reports of week+ response times — unacceptable when a client's agent breaks mid-day
- **Inbound optimization:** Bland is built for outbound campaigns; dental calls are inbound

**Retell AI** wins on inbound dental calls:
- ~600ms latency (industry-leading for inbound)
- Native appointment booking integrations
- HIPAA-eligible plans
- Better voice quality
- Comparable pricing at moderate volume

> **Alternative:** Synthflow AI has the strongest white-label features (custom domain, Stripe rebilling) but the agency plan ($1,400/mo) is only worth it at 8+ clients. Revisit Synthflow at that point.

- [IF CALENDAR INTEGRATION IS ACTIVE]: You have access to real-time availability. Book directly and confirm the slot.

- If caller describes: severe pain, swelling, abscess, broken tooth with bleeding, injury → say: "This sounds like it may need same-day attention. Let me connect you to our on-call team right now." → Transfer to {{EMERGENCY_NUMBER}}

- Ask: Full name, date of birth, phone number, email, insurance provider and member ID, reason for first visit, how they heard about the practice

- Always end with: "Thank you for calling {{PRACTICE_NAME}}. Have a great day!"

## Setup Call (Day 1 or 2, 30–45 min)

**Agenda:**

2. **(15 min)** Live test call together — call the agent, walk through scenarios

## What to Do When Something Breaks

### Agent not answering calls
1. Check Bland AI status page (status.bland.ai) — is there a platform outage?
2. Log in, check if agent is still active and not paused
3. Call test number — does it ring through?
4. If all clear on Bland side → call client, check their call forwarding settings
5. If outage: notify client within 30 min with ETA from Bland, offer credit

### Client reports wrong information given
1. Pull the specific call recording (client gives you date/time)
2. Listen to the full call
3. Identify what knowledge was missing or wrong
4. Update the knowledge base or system prompt
5. Respond to client: "Fixed — here's what the agent will say now: [new response]"
6. Always within same business day

### Client wants to cancel
1. Don't panic — ask: "What would need to be different for this to keep working for you?"
2. Common fix: they think the agent is replacing their receptionist (it's not — it's supplementing)
3. Offer: 1 month at 50% while you troubleshoot, or downgrade to after-hours only
4. If they still want to cancel: honor it gracefully, offboard per SOP

## The 4 Core Automations to Sell

### Automation 1: Appointment Reminder System (+$150–$200/month)
**Problem it solves:** No-shows cost a dental practice $200–$500 per missed slot. Most practices do manual reminder calls.

**What it does:**
- 72 hours before appointment: SMS + email reminder with 1-click confirm/reschedule
- 24 hours before: final SMS reminder
- 2 hours before: if unconfirmed, AI makes an automated confirmation call
- No-show detected: auto-sends reschedule link + adds to recall list

**Tools:** Make.com ($9/month) + Twilio ($15–$30/month for SMS) + their practice management API or webhook

**Your build time:** 3–4 hours first client, 1–2 hours each repeat

**ROI pitch:** "If this saves just 2 no-shows a month at $400 average, that's $800 recovered for $175."

### Automation 2: Review Generation Campaign (+$150/month)
**Problem it solves:** Most dental practices have 50–200 Google reviews. Practices with 500+ reviews rank dramatically higher in local search.

**What it does:**
- 2 hours after appointment: patient gets SMS: "How was your visit at [Practice]? [Thumbs up / Thumbs down]"
- If positive: direct link to Google review page
- If negative: routes to private feedback form (captures complaint before it goes public)
- Tracks review count monthly, sends client monthly report

**Tools:** Make.com + Twilio + Google Business API (free)

**Your build time:** 2–3 hours

**ROI pitch:** "A practice going from 80 to 300+ reviews typically sees 30–40% more new patient calls from Google."

---

### Automation 3: Patient Reactivation Campaign (+$250–$400, one-time or monthly)
**Problem it solves:** Every practice has hundreds of patients who haven't been in for 18+ months. Reactivating one = $1,500+ lifetime value.

**What it does:**
- Extract lapsed patient list from their practice management system (they export as CSV)
- AI-personalized SMS/email sequence over 3 weeks: "Hi [name], it's been a while — is now a good time to schedule your next cleaning?"
- Warm leads automatically handed off to front desk to confirm
- Track reactivated patients vs. campaign cost

**Tools:** Make.com + Twilio + Claude API for personalization

**Your build time:** 4–6 hours (one-time build + run per quarter)

**ROI pitch:** "A 200-patient reactivation campaign that converts 15% = 30 patients back. At $300 average visit value, that's $9,000. Campaign cost: $300–$400."

### Automation 4: New Patient Intake Automation (+$200–$300/month)
**Problem it solves:** New patients call, leave info with front desk or AI agent, then staff manually enter data into Dentrix/Eaglesoft. Takes 10–15 min per patient.

**What it does:**
- After a new patient inquiry (from your voice agent or web form), patient gets a link to a digital intake form
- Completed form data automatically enters their practice management system (via API or CSV import)
- Patient insurance info triggers automated benefits verification request
- Patient gets confirmation + "what to bring to your first visit" email

**Tools:** Make.com + Typeform ($29/month for client) + PMS integration

**Your build time:** 5–8 hours (depends on PMS)

**ROI pitch:** "10 new patients a month × 12 minutes saved each = 2 hours of front desk time back per month. And no data entry errors."
